Calendar Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026 · Contact: yueertang@gmail.com

Calendar is a personal time-tracking app. It is not sold to the public and does not include ads, analytics, or accounts of its own.

What the app stores

Tags, goals, things, locally created events, calendar links, and tag overrides stay on your devices. The same Apple ID syncs that data through iCloud Documents. Active work sessions and events read from the system calendar stay on the device that created them.

System calendars

If you grant calendar access, Calendar reads EventKit so it can show your existing events and write ones you create in the app. iCloud calendars already sync through Apple. The app does not send calendar data to any other server.

What we do not collect

There is no backend, crash reporter, or advertising SDK. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting data

Delete the app to remove the on-device copy. Remove the Calendar documents from iCloud Drive (or sign out of iCloud) to clear the synced copy. Revoke calendar access in iOS or macOS Settings at any time.
